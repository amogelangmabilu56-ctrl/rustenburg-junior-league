Rustenburg Junior League

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Rustenburg Junior League (RJL) app uses and how it is handled. The app shows league logs, fixtures, results and teams for the U11, U13, U15 and U17 age groups.

Using the app without logging in

Clubs, players, parents and supporters can view logs, fixtures, results and teams without an account. The app does not ask them for any personal information, does not track them and does not show advertising.

League information shown in the app

League administrators enter the following, which anyone using the app can see:

  • Team and club names, and the age group each team plays in
  • Player names and shirt numbers in each team's squad
  • Fixture dates, week numbers and match results

Because the league is for junior players, a parent or guardian can ask for a player's name to be changed or removed from the app by emailing the address below. It will be removed as soon as possible.

Administrator accounts

League administrators log in with an email address and password created for them by the main league administrator. These accounts are managed by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. Passwords are handled by Firebase and are not visible to the league. Administrator email addresses are only used to give access to the app and to send password reset emails when requested.

Where the information is stored

League information and administrator accounts are stored on Google Firebase. The app keeps a copy of the most recently loaded logs, fixtures and teams on the device so that they still show without a signal. This copy contains only the league information listed above.

Sharing

The league does not sell or share any information with third parties. Google provides the Firebase hosting and login services under its own privacy terms.

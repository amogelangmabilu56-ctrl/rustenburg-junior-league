Upload or update fixtures from Excel

Use the template: one row per club match, with Week No, Date, Home team, Away team, and the Home goals and Away goals for U11, U13, U15 and U17. Each row creates the game in every age group where both clubs have a team. Fill in both goals to record a score, or leave them blank for a game not played yet. Uploading again updates the same games, it does not add them twice. Team names must match the Teams tab.
